Monthly workforce and running average

Each bar is a month's average daily workforce (its man-days ÷ its reporting days in the date range), split by shift; the dark tick on a bar is that month's peak day. The dark line is the running average: man-days from the start of the date range to the month's last report, divided by the reporting days so far. The amber diamond is the month's planned average, where the labour report plans a trade. A reporting day is a date with any record in the labour report
